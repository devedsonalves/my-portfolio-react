Create one original pixel-art character portrait of this exact young man, in the cozy 16-bit farm RPG character portrait aesthetic associated with Stardew Valley. Preserve recognizable dark brown short side-swept textured hair with slightly longer fringe, dark brown eyes, straight thick eyebrows, light warm skin, clean-shaven face, and plain black crew-neck T-shirt from the reference. Friendly subtle closed-mouth smile. Head and upper shoulders only, facing almost straight forward, subtly three-quarter like an NPC dialog portrait. Large expressive head, simplified distinctive facial features, crisp square pixel clusters and stepped edges, dark brown pixel outline, restrained warm earthy palette with 3-4 flat shades per material. Authentic hand-crafted low-resolution pixel art on a consistent approximately 64x64 logical grid, nearest-neighbor upscaled, NOT a smooth illustration with a pixel filter. Square composition with the head occupying most of the canvas and small transparent padding around the silhouette. True transparent background and alpha; no scene, no text, no letters, no watermark, no frame, no circle, no props, no sprite sheet. Readable silhouette at 32px and 48px icon size. Deliver one polished final avatar.
